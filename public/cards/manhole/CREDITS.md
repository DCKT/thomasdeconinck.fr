# Manhole cover photos

Most photos come from Wikimedia Commons, category
[Colored manhole covers in Japan](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Colored_manhole_covers_in_Japan).
Each was cropped square around the cover and resized. Photos under a CC BY-SA licence
stay under that licence in their cropped form.

The collab covers (Pokémon, Gundam, Expo 2025) come from Flickr under Creative Commons
licences. The "NoDerivatives" ones (CC BY-NC-ND) are not cropped: the whole photo is
shown, letterboxed on a dark background. The licence covers the photo only; the
characters remain © their owners (©Pokémon, ©Sotsu·Sunrise, ©Expo 2025).

The per-photo author, licence and source page are listed in
`app/components/konbini/cards-data.ts` and shown on every card in the binder.
